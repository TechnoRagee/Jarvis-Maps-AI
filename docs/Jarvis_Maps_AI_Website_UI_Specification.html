5. Download Experience & SmartScreen Walkthrough

To ensure downloaders have zero friction opening the software, the website will display an interactive Trust & Verification box directly beneath the download button:

Step 1: Download & Extract

User clicks "Download for Windows" to receive JarvisMapsAI_Windows_x64.zip, right-clicks and chooses Extract All.

Step 2: Windows SmartScreen

If Windows shows "Unknown Publisher", click "More info" followed by "Run anyway" to launch.

Step 3: Enter Passcode

User launches JarvisMapsAI.exe and unlocks with master passcode Jarvis2026! to begin work.
